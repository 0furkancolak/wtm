# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful, constructive, and considerate; focus criticism on ideas and code; respect privacy and differing experience levels; and stop behavior when asked.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's private contact channel. Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect reporter confidentiality where possible, and apply proportionate corrective action.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly. It is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
